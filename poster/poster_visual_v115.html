Failures localize and compound

The full-grid maps locate the remaining tail. A rollout comparison then shows how an initially small recovery delay accumulates over time.

Where do the five trained actors fail?

white: all five succeed · color: number that fail
Pure RL: 497 failures across 208 initial states. RL + supervised: nine failures across eight initial states. The same difficult regions recur across retraining seeds.

One difficult start, four time points

five translucent rods show five independently trained actors
By step 64: all five mixed actors are near the stored-reference return; none of the five pure-RL actors are. Pure RL eventually reaches upright, but the delayed swing-up loses too much return.
Inside the trained networks: FastSACN8 and SACN8 actors output the torque bound on 87% of tested states. At sampled failure states, clipping preserves only 7% of a local critic-gradient step. Full-range Q-search ranks feasible torques directly, avoiding this projection bottleneck.
Interpretation: reward training learns useful critic rankings, and reflection plus Q-search recover 770 of 1,267 pure-RL failures. Supervised actions at actor-visited states make the multi-step recovery path more repeatable across retraining seeds.
References: SAC, Haarnoja et al. 2018 · DAgger, Ross et al. 2011 · SimbaV2, Lee et al. 2025 · SACn, Łyskawa et al. 2025.
