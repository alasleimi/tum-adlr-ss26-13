## Summary

The question and two-part answer are immediate. Reliability, task success, and strict reference wins are explicitly separated. The story moves coherently from methods to evidence, seed behavior, ablations, and mechanism. Visible arithmetic is consistent, including the 664 near-reference gap, the +170 task-success gap, and the -733 strict-win difference. The PDF is one correctly sized landscape A0 page with clean margins and no visible clipping.

## Remaining issues

- The opening should describe one method or policy design across five separately trained actors, rather than one policy.
- The headline is stronger than warranted by retrospective selection from 404 evaluations without held-out confirmation.
- The mixed and pure 100k ledgers use different kinds of learning events and should not look directly interchangeable.
- Several labels, captions, and ledger notes are small relative to available space.
- Normalized residuals, FastSACN8, reset support, and reference-like actions need plainer definitions.
- Grid ranges, seed protocol, and reference construction are not fully specified on the poster.

## Highest-impact revisions

1. Make retrospective five-seed grid selection explicit near the headline.
2. Enlarge small plot and ledger text where possible.
3. Clarify the non-equivalence of the two 100k ledgers and define remaining method jargon.
