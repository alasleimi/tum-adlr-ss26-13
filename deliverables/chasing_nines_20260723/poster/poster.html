1 · Method

Two clean routes to one frozen policy

RL + supervised

1TRAINSimbaV2 learns full-circle actions from stored dynamic-programming and controller references.
2TRAINDAgger labels states visited by the actor. Three rounds add 30k transitions; a 20k follow-up prioritizes measured failures.
3DEPLOYA shared 50k reward-only critic checks actor ± {0, 0.05, 0.10}. Its mostly one-step FastSACN8 target includes a 0.775% eight-step auxiliary. Both critics must approve.

Pure RL

1TRAINSimbaV2 SAC learns from reward for 100,000 transitions.
2DEPLOYProject the actor through left-right symmetry: asym(s) = [π(s) − π(Ms)] / 2.
3DEPLOYSearch 41 torques in [−2, 2]. Replace asym only when both critics predict an improvement above 0.005.

5 · Mechanism diagnostic

Critic uncertainty is larger than the local signal

Pairwise action ordering84.4%
Harmful tested Q proposals19.0%
Tail signal problem: median twin-critic disagreement is 2.43 times the median local Q range. Typical rankings help, but harmful tested proposals occur 19.0% of the time on these targeted hard states.

Strict wins tell a different story. Pure RL beats the stored reference in 18.4% of trials, compared with 12.6% for the mixed method. Reliability and strict improvement are different operating points.

Diagnostic scope: five seeds, 99 hard states, and 21 actions per state. Pairwise ordering compares Q with realized counterfactual return.

Limits: one deterministic task and selected grid campaigns. Mechanism evidence is targeted, not causal or held out.

Take-home: mixed training suppresses seed-dependent failures. Pure RL often finds excellent actions, but its critic cannot gate tail corrections reliably enough.
