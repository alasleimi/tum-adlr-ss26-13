02Supervision removes most failure regions

All five mixed actors succeed on 2,493 of 2,501 starts. For pure RL, all five succeed on 2,293, outcomes vary by seed on 195, and all five fail on 13.

03The gap appears during the closed-loop recovery

From the same hard start, the reference and mixed actor are upright by step 64. The raw pure-RL actor is still drifting and finishes 103.7 return points lower.

Mechanistic story:The critics often identify a better immediate action. Supervision also teaches the actor what to do after that action changes the next state.
