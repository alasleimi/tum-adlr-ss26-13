The measurements are consistent with an actor-side bottleneck: critic directions are often useful, while saturation removes most of the local update. Scoring 41 torques bypasses projection and raises near-reference success from 92.363% to 96.026%.
RL plus supervision leaves 9 near-reference failures in 12,505 trials. Pure RL reaches 96.026% near-reference success and 22.823% strict wins; its internal measurements are consistent with actor saturation and clipping in the remaining tail.
