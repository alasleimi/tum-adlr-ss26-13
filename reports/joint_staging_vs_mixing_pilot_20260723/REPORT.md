# Completed simultaneous BC and SAC seed-0 pilot

## Scope

This is a 25,000-step, single-seed diagnostic. It starts a SimbaV2 actor and SimbaV2 distributional critics from random initialization. Static reference-anchor behavior cloning is active throughout actor training. The stochastic SAC actor term becomes active after step 6,000, so both terms share one actor optimizer update for the rest of training.

The critic uses eight equally weighted SACn targets. This is not the properly weighted FastSACN8 target used by the selected mixed deployment critic. The supervised samples are static reference anchors rather than learner-state DAgger data.

## Fixed-grid result

Both terminal actors were evaluated without Q-search on the same 61 by 41 initial-state grid.

| Seed-0 method | Near reference | Task success | Strict wins | Mean return |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Reward-only SACn8 control | 1,757 / 2,501, 70.252% | 1,931 / 2,501, 77.209% | 31 / 2,501, 1.240% | -159.710 |
| Simultaneous BC + SACn8 | 2,443 / 2,501, 97.681% | 2,336 / 2,501, 93.403% | 164 / 2,501, 6.557% | -140.050 |
| Joint minus control | +686, +27.429 points | +405, +16.194 points | +133, +5.318 points | +19.660 |

This comparison shows that the supervised term improves this seed relative to the matched reward-only control. It does not show that simultaneous optimization is better than behavior cloning alone or a staged behavior-cloning-then-SAC schedule because those controls were not completed.

## Gradient and representation diagnostic

The 20 logged joint intervals from step 6,000 through step 25,000 show:

- Median weighted BC-to-SAC actor-gradient norm ratio: 37.19
- Minimum and maximum logged ratio: 31.49 to 42.58
- Median interval-mean BC-SAC gradient cosine: 0.075
- Individual minibatch cosine range: -0.943 to 0.935
- Maximum dormant fraction across logged actor and critic layers at the registered threshold: 0

The simultaneous update is therefore dominated numerically by behavior cloning even after the configured BC coefficient decays from 10 to 4. Individual minibatches include both aligned and conflicting directions. A scientifically useful next comparison should add gradient normalization and conflict handling, but must retain BC-only, reward-only, staged, and unmodified simultaneous controls.
